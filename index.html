Cặp sách

Pack your school bag

Bring

  • A laptop with an AI assistant: Claude, ChatGPT or Copilot.
  • Jira access for one person per team, with a token or an MCP connection. The setup guide arrives 2 days before.
  • Your team name. Teams are mixed: one senior, one manager, and one to three juniors.

Know

  • All data is made up. The starter kit has a sample sprint, 50 reviews, a meeting transcript and more.
  • Write tickets only to the SBX sandbox project, never to a real squad board.
  • No real user data in any AI tool. No phone numbers, account IDs or transaction IDs.
